PLACE YOUR COMPILED APK HERE:
==============================
Build your release APK locally in Android Studio using:
   .\gradlew.bat clean assembleRelease

Then copy 'app/build/outputs/apk/release/app-release.apk' into this folder
and rename it to 'god4xe_esports.apk'.

Alternatively, you can upload your APK to Google Drive, Telegram, or Mediafire,
and paste the public download link into the Website Admin Panel under:
   Admin -> Settings -> Android APK Download URL
